About Us

Angel Event Solution is an event company established in 2022.

Since our beginning, we have focused on creating events that feel personal, organized, and truly memorable for every client.

From concept planning and venue setup to guest coordination and on-ground execution, our team handles every detail with care. We combine creative ideas, disciplined planning, and reliable vendor management to deliver smooth events from start to finish.

Whether it is a private celebration, a wedding function, or a large brand activity, our goal is always the same: high-quality execution, transparent communication, and complete client satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I book Angel Event Solution for my event?

For weddings and large events, we recommend booking at least 2 to 4 months in advance. For smaller events, 3 to 6 weeks is usually enough.

Do you handle both planning and on-ground execution?

Yes. We manage everything from concept and vendor coordination to setup, timeline management, and full event-day execution.

Can you work within a fixed budget?

Absolutely. We create a plan based on your budget and priorities, then suggest the best options to maximize quality without overspending.

Do you provide services outside my city?

Yes, we provide event and advertising support across multiple cities in India. Share your location and we will guide you on logistics.

How can I get a quote for my event?

You can submit the consultation form on this page or contact us by call or WhatsApp. We will connect with you to understand your requirements and share a custom quote.
